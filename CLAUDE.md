# Working rules for this repository

## Design: never use these (applies to every project, video, page and deck)

These are the marks of generic AI-made design. None of them goes into anything we make here.

- Coloured stripes on rounded cards
- Borders around every block
- Coloured italic text used as an accent
- An all-caps label above every heading
- Badges and dots that mean nothing
- Monospace labels and inputs
- Numbers written as words
- Long titles stuffed with nouns
- Emojis used as icons
- Placeholders where real avatars, logos and images should be
- Overstuffed screens
- Visual metaphors explained with labels
- Demos made of text
- Glass cards
- The same corner radius and shadow on everything
- Light grey text on white
- Animation and motion that say nothing
- A heavy appear animation on every block
- Proof nobody can check
- Purple gradients, gradients in general, neon glow, grid lines on dark backgrounds

What to do instead: use the brand's real assets (logo, product photos, fonts), dark text on light backgrounds, one idea per screen, plain cuts or simple fades unless the motion itself shows something (a pour, a cut, steam), and only claims that can be checked against a source.
