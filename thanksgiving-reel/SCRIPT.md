# Served with thanks

A 24-second Instagram Reel for Polygood® by The Good Plastic Company, for US Thanksgiving on Thursday 26 November 2026. Built in Remotion first, with the cooking shots to be remade in Blender next (see [BLENDER.md](BLENDER.md)).

Format: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 726 frames. Cover: `out/reel-cover.png`.

## The idea

We cooked something for you. Every dish on a Thanksgiving table is a Polygood pattern. Each course starts on the pattern the way polygood.com shows it: the real product photo, the name, and what the panel is made from. Then the sample comes off the page and gets cooked. It is poured into a bowl, chopped into a dish, or shaped and roasted on a platter. By the end the table is set, and the plates, the runner, the napkins and even the salt are Polygood too.

Then the turn. A material company only brings the ingredients. All year, the people who did the cooking were the architects, interior designers, specifiers, fabricators, brands and distributors who put Polygood into their projects. The film thanks them for every one.

Only the brightest, most appetising dishes are on the menu. Gravy, stuffing and green beans were cut because their patterns read dark or grey on screen.

## The menu

| Course | Pattern | Made from (polygood.com) | How it is cooked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cranberry sauce | Translucent Burgundy | Recycled CD cases | Poured into a glass bowl, simmers |
| Mashed potatoes | Marbellous | Recycled refrigerators | Cut into cubes, dropped in, mashed, butter on top (Translucent Orange) |
| Cornbread | Translucent Glitter Gold | Recycled CD cases | Batter poured into the skillet, baked, cut into squares |
| Pumpkin pie | Translucent Orange, with Milky Way cream | Recycled CD cases | Filling poured into a Translucent Glitter Gold crust |
| Roast turkey | Translucent Bronze | Recycled CD cases | Shaped on the platter and roasted, with Translucent Glitter Green sage and Translucent Burgundy cranberries |

Bronze suits the turkey twice over: the panel has the colour of a roasted bird, and Bronze is the classic American heritage turkey breed.

## The table

Tabletop: Frost. Runner: Gossamer. Plates: Timeless Duo. Napkins: Salt Dune. Glasses: Translucent Clear. Serveware and candles: Vintage Pearl. Centrepiece: Potpourri. Platter: Terrazzo Nuovo. Place cards: White Lollipop. Salt: Fleur de Sel.
