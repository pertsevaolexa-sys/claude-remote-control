## 01 — Karaoke for a Good Cause

**Tabasamu Austria · Vienna · June 2026**

A zero-budget charity night at G5 Vienna for Tabasamu Austria — 160 guests, a live band, karaoke and a donation box.

On 1 June 2026 our Event Marketing group ran Karaoke for a Good Cause at G5, under the U-Bahn arches in Vienna's 19th district: a free-entry charity night for Tabasamu Austria, a non-profit that funds educational projects for children in East Africa. Karaoke, a live set by Elfsechzig, a lucky draw and a donation box at the door.

The constraint that shaped everything was money, because we had none. The whole evening ran as a zero-budget concept covered through sponsoring and partnerships — more than twenty companies contributed prizes or in-kind support — Raiffeisen Bank Austria, NEOH and many more — so every euro collected on the night went to the cause.

We aimed for 150 guests and counted around 160. With no media budget, the reach came from borrowed trust: one visual identity across poster, flyers and Instagram, carried by reposts from the band, the venue, the ÖH and Tabasamu itself.

*A group project during the Event Marketing course at FHWien der WKW, Vienna. Photography by Claudy Visions.*

## 02 — Museum Connections, Paris

**Museum Booster · Vienna · January 2026**

Launch communication for Museum Booster's stand at Museum Connections in Paris: messaging, printed materials and the public introduction of the Museum Booster Academy.

Preparation for Museum Booster's stand at Museum Connections, the museum trade fair in Paris: the messaging, the printed materials and the launch assets the Museum Booster Academy needed for its first public appearance.

The concept on the stand is called Don't Panic. Museum leaders work under shrinking budgets and public scrutiny, and most leadership programmes sell them urgency. This one does the opposite: reassurance first, then a clear invitation into four two-day workshops in Vienna.

The visual world for the expo borrowed from art the audience already knows: Edvard Munch's *The Scream* became the face of Don't Panic, and a still from Bernardo Bertolucci's *The Dreamers* — the run through the Louvre — carried the same energy onto the materials we brought to the trade fair.

**In numbers**

| 4 | two-day workshops |
| 25 | seats per cohort |
| 4,500+ | professionals in reach |

## 06 — The Conscious Toolkit

**Living Lab 040 · Eindhoven**
