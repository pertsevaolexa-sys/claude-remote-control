Museum Booster Academy · Vienna, 2025–26

Marketing for a training programme: LinkedIn strategy, Eventbrite pages, Squarespace components, launch assets for four courses.

The launch concept is called Don’t Panic. Museum leaders work under shrinking budgets and public scrutiny, and most leadership programmes sell them urgency. This one does the opposite: a nine-phase launch built on reassurance, opening with a public LinkedIn discussion on museum leadership towards 2030 and ending in four two-day workshops in Vienna, 25 seats each.
