## Hotel interior trends, 2026 (Dezeen)

Architectural Digest blocks automated access, so it is not used here. Dezeen findings come
from search-result summaries of these articles.

| Project | What to take from it |
|---|---|

| [Aethos London Shoreditch, Astet Studio](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/08/27/aethos-hotel-shoreditch-london-astet-studio/) | Matte materials that absorb light, hand-finished timber, Japanese carpentry details, low seating and big rugs, blackened wood in rooms |
| [Arthouse Glasgow, A-nrd](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/08/12/arthouse-glasgow-hotel-scotland-a-nrd/) | Olive colour-drenched lounge (walls, ceiling, joinery), zinc and dark timber, local glass lamps |
| [Jam Hotel Ghent, Studio Lionel Jadot](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/03/01/lionel-jadot-jam-hotel-ghent-artists-atelier/) | Keep the building's layers; reclaimed timber floors; furniture from salvage; modular beds that come apart; mycelium shades |
| [The Hoxton Shoreditch, 20th anniversary](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/02/09/the-hoxton-hotel-20th-anniversary-revamp-shoreditch/) | Restored and second-hand furniture; dark timber, velvet and bouclé |
| [Crafted at Powdermills, House of Dré](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/02/23/crafted-powdermills-hotel-house-of-dre/) | Named local makers: timber furniture, clay lamps, willow host stand; colour drenching |
| [The Dean Berlin, Rachael Gowdridge](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/02/26/rachael-gowdridge-the-dean-hotel-berlin/) | Vintage pieces with bespoke veneered joinery; avoiding a "generic hotel feel" |
| [The Dean Munich, Tatjana von Stein](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/08/17/tatjana-von-stein-the-dean-munich/) | Sunken conversation area around a marble bar; tubular shelving in rooms |
| [Kimpton Ashbel, Busta Studio](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/08/14/busta-studio-kimpton-ashbel-hotel/) | Limed oak arches, arch chairs, bouclé and linen, hand-cut mosaic |
| [Kimpton Era, INC](https://www.dezeen.com/2026/10/03/kimpton-era-hotel-new-york-city-inc/) | Compact "berth" rooms with one white-oak wall for bed, desk and storage; fluted concrete; terracotta and glazed ceramics |
| [Hotel Sevilla, Zeller & Moye](https://www.dezeen.com/awards/2026/shortlists/hotel-sevilla/) | The building as a palimpsest: decay kept, new concrete and metal added |
| [Fairmont Tokyo, BAR Studio](https://www.dezeen.com/2025/12/21/fairmont-tokyo-interior-bar-studio/) | Woven-look wallcoverings, ash, quartzite, glass bricks |
