# October 2026 newsletter analysis: the numbers

Source: Mailchimp analytics for the audience "The Good Plastic Company Newsletter", pulled 12–13 October 2026. Subject of the analysis: campaign 12774360, "The Polygood® Project Edit: Autumn 2026", sent Friday 9 October 2026 at about 14:07 UTC (16:07 Amsterdam) from news@thegoodplasticcompany.com, compared with the five regular sends before it.

The split between people and mail filters is a behavioural classification made during the analysis; Mailchimp does not label these clicks. Confidence is medium. Treat findings based on the roughly 47 real clickers as direction, and re-check them against later sends.

## 2. People versus mail filters

| Group | Addresses | Clicks |
|---|---|---|
| People | 47 | 78 |
| Unclear | 9 | 45 |
| Mail filters | 99 | 1,210 (91%) |

How the filters showed themselves:

- 85 addresses made their first click within about 2 minutes of sending.
- 60 addresses clicked exactly Salmon Terra ×6 and de Bijenkorf ×4, about 7 seconds apart: the same scan pass, repeated.
- 3 addresses clicked all 17 links in the same second (43 clicks each).
- Click locations were cloud data centres: Ashburn, Dulles and Boydton (Virginia), Boardman (Oregon), Columbus (Ohio).
- Mailchimp's bot-click flag marked none of them.
- The same addresses "click" every newsletter the same way: 79 of the 82 addresses that also clicked two or more of the previous five sends are filters.

The people behaved differently: 15 of 47 clicked on a phone, 26 made their first click more than an hour after sending, and clicks spread over three days.

## 7. List health

- Subscribed contacts: 10,897 (1 Apr 2026) → 10,165 (1 Oct 2026), down 732 (6.7%). Unsubscribed contacts rose by 244; the rest were removed from the audience, mostly in August.
- Total contacts on 1 Oct 2026: 12,491 (10,165 subscribed, 2,326 unsubscribed).
- No opens in the last 90 days: 7,522 subscribers (74%); in the last 180 days: 6,745 (66%).
- Signup source: every delivered contact is "List Import"; nobody joined through a form.
- Signup cohorts: 2024 import 5,383 contacts (17.2% open rate), 2025 imports 4,576 (20.0%), 2026: 55 (25.5%).
- 64% of contacts have no country on their profile.
- Bounces above 2% on all six sends; on this send 280 of 286 bounces were on company domains.

## 9. How each finding became a rule

| Finding | Rule in SKILL.md |
|---|---|
| 91% of clicks were mail filters; Mailchimp flagged none | Judge results by people-only clicks and Google Analytics sessions; pick A/B winners manually |
| People clicked projects 41 times, products 11 | Lead with project stories; put colours inside stories with a sample link |
| Readers chose projects from their own region | Regional versions with a local hero project |
| Friday afternoon send split readers over the weekend | Send Tuesday–Thursday morning |
| 74% inactive for 90 days; list only from imports | Exclude 180-day inactive from regular sends; separate re-engagement; grow the list through forms |
| Large firms with zero opens | Seed-test junk and quarantine at a large firm; check SPF, DKIM, DMARC; real text, not images |
| No preview text; brand-and-season subject line | Four subject options with different angles; preview text always filled |
| Repeat "engaged" clickers are filters | Never build engaged segments from clicks |
| Sustainability and social links barely clicked | Keep them in the footer |
