# Inside the award-winning Vienna hotel where the washstands used to be fridges

> Source: Notion page exported 27 Oct 2026 (`hotelarticle_.zip`). Text unchanged except the ending, where the two calls to action are merged into one question as requested. Before publishing, check the "fridges" claim in the title (see `02-facts-and-sources.md`, item 1).

On 8 October 2026, the Circular Living rooms at Henriette Stadthotel Vienna won the zeig profil award 2026. Their washstands are Polygood®, fitted in 2025, and after more than a year of guests co-owner Verena Brandtner-Pastuszyn says they are in "impeccable condition".

Henriette is a family-run, 68-room four-star on Vienna's Praterstraße, owned by Verena Brandtner-Pastuszyn and Georg Pastuszyn. In the 2025 rebuild, every material in the new Circular Living rooms had to be low in emissions and removable for reuse or recycling at the next refit. The floors are reclaimed parquet, the partition walls pressed straw, the carpets woven from old fishing nets.

The washstands had to meet the same rules, match rooms that each have their own colour scheme, and take daily hotel use.

> "I loved the idea of turning old fridges into something high-quality and extraordinary. The vibrant colours and pleasant texture won me over very quickly."

The hotel chose Polygood® in three patterns: Sapphire Terrazzo, Emerald Ghost and White Terrazzo, each picking up its room's colours.

## A year of guests and steam

A hotel washstand meets water, soap and cosmetics from a new guest every night or two. At Henriette it is then cleaned with steam and no chemical products, a house rule since 2020. Compared with the ceramic basins in the hotel's other rooms, Verena's verdict is simple:

> "It works perfectly and definitely doesn't require any extra effort."

## A design guests want to talk about

Most guests can't tell what the washstands are made of, so the hotel made it part of its guided tours: staff ask visitors to guess the material, then tell them.

> "It's the story that fascinates our guests."

The story has travelled well beyond the hotel. gast.at, GASTRO.at, globalmagazin and Change Maker Hotels all wrote about the washstands made from old fridges. In October, the jury of the zeig profil award, the annual prize of the Austrian news magazine profil for forward-looking projects, chose the Circular Living rooms from 272 entries in its Lebensräume (living spaces) category.

## Certified to the core

Henriette holds the Austrian and EU Ecolabels and took its new rooms through GreenSign Circular certification, so every material needed independent proof behind it. Polygood® came with that proof.

> "Polygood's certificates gave us exactly what we needed to make these rooms fully sustainable, from the floor to the washstand. They made our own certification easier, and that was one of the reasons we chose it."

## An easy choice to make again

The washstands look beautiful, have kept their condition through a year of guests and steam, and give the hotel a story people remember. To make fitting just as easy for the next hotel, we now cut, edge and CNC-finish washstands to the hotel's drawings, basin and tap cut-outs included, so they arrive ready to install.

Asked whether she would choose Polygood® again, Verena doesn't hesitate:

> "Definitely. We're very happy with them!"
