This edition follows one worktop through its life in four chapters: shaped in the workshop, fitted in the kitchen, lived in at home and shared at work. Each chapter is a real kitchen in Polygood® and shows what the material does at that stage.

Shaped in the workshop
Private home, Paris

Curves are hard in most worktop materials: they are built from pieces, and every joint is a line to keep clean. Samji Studio heated an Emerald Ghost panel until it softened, bent it into a curve and made the table to match. A bend instead of a joint.

Fitted in the kitchen
Maison Kyka, Paris

Polygood is solid all the way through, so the front edge of a worktop needs no edging strip and can become part of the design. For two compact Maison Kyka flats, Samji Studio made the Terrazzo Nuovo worktops. In one, the edge is shaped into a soft wave that picks up the curves of the wooden handles, so worktop and cabinets read as one design.

Lived in at home
Station Road, UK

A white worktop is the one clients hesitate over. In our stain test with 20 everyday agents, most left no trace on Polygood, and a mark that stays can be sanded out. WUNSCH fitted this one in White Lollipop. Daily care is a pH-neutral cleaner, with a trivet for hot pans.

Shared at work
Commerzbank, Amsterdam

In an office, the pantry is where the day happens: coffee between meetings, lunch, a queue at the sink. For the Amsterdam office of Commerzbank, one of Germany's largest banks, CBRE Design Collective chose Polygood Timeless Duo Blue for the pantry countertops. It absorbs no moisture, so the sink can sit directly under the countertop. It is tested for food contact and carries a 10-year limited warranty for interior use.
