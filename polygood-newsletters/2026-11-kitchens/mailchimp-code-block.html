This edition follows one worktop through its life in four chapters: shaped in the workshop, fitted in the kitchen, lived in every day and renewed at the next refit. Each chapter is a real kitchen in Polygood® and shows what the material does at that stage.
 
The life of a worktop
01 Shaped  ·  02 Fitted  ·  03 Lived in  ·  04 Renewed
01 Shaped  ·  The curve
Private home, Paris

Curves are hard in most worktop materials: they are built from pieces, and every joint is a line to keep clean. Samji Studio heated an Emerald Ghost panel until it softened, bent it into a curve and made the table to match. A bend instead of a joint.

02 Fitted  ·  The edge
Maison Kyka, Paris

On many worktops the front edge is a strip glued over the core, and it is often the first thing to lift. Polygood is solid all the way through and needs no edge protection. Samji Studio made the Terrazzo Nuovo worktops for two Maison Kyka flats, and in one cut the edge into a wave and left it bare.

03 Lived in  ·  Stains and care
Station Road, UK

A white worktop is the one clients hesitate over. In our stain test with 20 everyday agents, most left no trace on Polygood, and a mark that stays can be sanded out. WUNSCH fitted this one in White Lollipop. Daily care is a pH-neutral cleaner, with a trivet for hot pans.

04 Renewed  ·  Water and refit
Commerzbank, Amsterdam

Polygood absorbs no moisture, so in Commerzbank's pantry, designed by CBRE Design Collective, the sink sits under the Timeless Duo Blue countertop with the cut-out edge left bare. When the pantry is refitted, the countertops can come back to us through our take-back programme. Polygood is 100% recyclable.
