The Polygood® Project Edit
Kitchens edition: four projects, four jobs a worktop has to do, and an open call to kitchen makers.
 
A kitchen worktop does more work than any other surface in a building: water at the sink, spills, knocks on the edge and years of daily cleaning. Each project below shows one of those jobs and how Polygood® handles it. If you make kitchens, this edition is also an open call to work with us.

An edge with nothing to peel off
01 · The edge
Maison Kyka, Paris
Application: Kitchen worktops in two apartments

Samji Studio made the Terrazzo Nuovo worktops for two compact Maison Kyka apartment kitchens in Paris, one in the 7th arrondissement and one near Saint-Germain in the 6th.

The challenge.  In a small kitchen the front edge takes the knocks: hips, bags, wet hands. On many worktops that edge is a strip glued over the core, and it is often the first thing to lift.

How Polygood answers it.  Polygood is the same material all the way through, so a worktop needs no edge protection. In the 6th, Samji cut the edge into a wave that echoes the cabinet handles and left it bare.

Over the years.  There is no strip to come away, and a mark on a satin finish can be sanded out rather than the worktop replaced.

A curve made with heat, not joints
02 · The curve
A private home, Paris
Application: Kitchen worktop and matching table

A private client in Paris asked Samji Studio for a kitchen worktop and a table to match. Samji made both in Emerald Ghost, a flecked sea green. It is the only thermoformed kitchen in our project archive so far.

The challenge.  Curves are hard in most worktop materials. They are built up from pieces or left out, and every joint is another line to keep clean.

How Polygood answers it.  Polygood can be thermoformed. Samji heated the panel until it softened, shaped it over a mould and let it cool into a curve, in the same pattern as the table.

Over the years.  A bend instead of a joint means one less line for dirt to collect in, and the panel holds its new shape once it has cooled.

A white worktop that is easy to look after
03 · Stains and care
Station Road, UK
Application: Kitchen worktop

WUNSCH, cabinetmakers who design and make bespoke kitchens in their Lewes workshop in Sussex, cut and fitted this worktop on Station Road in White Lollipop: a white ground with small flecks of colour.

The challenge.  A white worktop is the one clients hesitate over. Everyday spills show on a pale surface, and they worry it will look tired in a few years.

How Polygood answers it.  In our stain test with 20 everyday agents, most left no trace on Polygood. Daily cleaning is a pH-neutral cleaner or a little washing-up liquid, nothing stronger.

Over the years.  A mark that does stay can be sanded out, so the surface is renewed rather than replaced, and the panels carry a 10-year limited warranty for interior use.

Built for the sink and the next refit
04 · Water and the next refit
Commerzbank, Amsterdam
Application: Pantry countertops

CBRE Design Collective designed the staff pantry in Commerzbank's Amsterdam office in Zuidas, with countertops in Timeless Duo Blue: a white ground with blue flecks.

The challenge.  An office pantry is used by many people all day. Water sits around the sink, food is prepared on the counter, and an office fit-out rarely lasts as long as the building.

How Polygood answers it.  Polygood absorbs no moisture, so the steel sink is mounted under the countertop with the edge of the cut-out left bare. The material is tested for food contact under EU Regulation 10/2011.

Over the years.  When the pantry is refitted, the countertops can come back to us through our take-back programme. Polygood is a single material and 100% recyclable.

Design: CBRE Design Collective · Client: Commerzbank AG
