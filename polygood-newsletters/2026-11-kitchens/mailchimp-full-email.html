The Polygood® Project Edit
This edition is an open call to kitchen makers. Two independent workshops show what a joint project with us looks like.
 
Every kitchen in this edition was made by an independent workshop: Samji Studio in Paris and WUNSCH in the UK. We want more collaborations like these, so this edition doubles as an open call. If you make kitchens, this is what a joint project with Polygood® looks like.
Partner with us on a kitchen
 
Terrazzo Nuovo worktops for two Paris flats
Made with Samji Studio
Maison Kyka, Paris
Application: Kitchen worktops in two apartments

Samji Studio, a Paris workshop that makes furniture to measure, made the worktops for two Maison Kyka apartment kitchens: one in the 7th arrondissement, one near Saint-Germain in the 6th. Both are in Terrazzo Nuovo, a pale terrazzo pattern with fine dark flecks. In the 7th it sits on timber cabinets around a black sink. In the 6th, Samji cut the front edge into a wave that echoes the cabinet handles.

Fabrication and design: Samji Studio · Design: Kyka · Client: Maison Kyka · Photography: Samji Studio

An Emerald Ghost kitchen with a thermoformed curve
Made with Samji Studio
A private home, Paris
Application: Kitchen worktop and matching table

A private client in Paris asked Samji Studio for a kitchen worktop and a table to match. Samji made both in Emerald Ghost, a flecked sea green, set against timber cabinets and herringbone parquet. Part of the work was thermoformed: Samji heated the panel until it softened, shaped it over a mould and let it cool into a curve. It is the only thermoformed kitchen in our project archive so far.

Fabrication: Samji Studio · Client: Private · Photography: Samji Studio

A White Lollipop worktop for a Station Road kitchen
Made with WUNSCH
Station Road, UK
Application: Kitchen worktop

WUNSCH, cabinetmakers in the UK, cut and fitted the worktop for this kitchen on Station Road in White Lollipop, a white ground with small flecks of colour. The flecks pick up the rest of the room: mustard-yellow fronts, pale birch ply, green glazed tiles and the reds and oranges on the open shelf. WUNSCH also photographed the finished kitchen.

Cabinetmaking and photography: WUNSCH

Beyond the kitchen: tables, chairs and lights
Workplace
Rootz, the Netherlands
Application: Tables, chairs, lampshades and a chandelier

Rootz is a knowledge and innovation centre for the tree nursery sector in West Brabant, a place for work, meetings and research. In February 2025 All Art Design furnished it in Polygood: tables and chairs in Terrazzo Nuovo and Sapphire Terrazzo, lampshades and a chandelier in Aqua Drift. The panels form both the surface and the structure, so the furniture needs no separate frame, and the blue shades soften the light that passes through them.

Design and fabrication: All Art Design · Photography: Jesper Productions · Client: Rootz
