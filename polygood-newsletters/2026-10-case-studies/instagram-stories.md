## 0. Opening

**Four new projects on our website**

On Dam Square in Amsterdam, De Bijenkorf's mannequins and bags now stand on Salmon Terra plinths. In Vienna, the Henriette Stadthotel chose three Polygood® patterns for the washstands in its new Circular Living rooms. In Paris, the teal kitchenette at Orion's office has worktops in Emerald Ghost. In Taipei, ONE DUST Studio designed and made tables, chairs, benches and shelves in five of our patterns.

## Notes on the credits

- De Bijenkorf, Orion and ONE DUST: the credits are the ones in our case PDFs. Public sources agree. Random Studio worked with the store's design team on the women's floor, and Fiction Factory was the main builder of the fixtures. Aura and Samji both list Orion among their projects.
- Henriette: the case PDF credits only the photographer. Vienna trade press (globalmagazin, GASTRO.at, Change Maker Hotels) names Jürgen Kalcher-Rothenstein and Josef Göbel as the designers of the 2025 conversion. No public source names who made or installed the washstands, so fill in that line from our order records, or drop it.
- Left out on purpose:
  - STOOFF Interior Projects and Beersnielsen worked on the Bijenkorf department, but nothing ties them to the Polygood stands.
  - Tischlerei Göbel built the Henriette wardrobes, not the washstands.
  - Rittweger + Team were the hotel's circularity consultants, not its designers.
- Handles: @hotel.henriette comes from the case PDF. Find the other studios' handles on their own profiles before tagging.
- Wording: no frame says what the material used to be, the "Austria's first" claim is attributed to the owners, and there are no performance claims.
