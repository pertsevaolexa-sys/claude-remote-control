## ONE DUST Studio, Taipei

**Slug:** `one-dust-studio-taipei`  
**SEO title:** ONE DUST Studio, Taipei - Polygood by The Good Plastic Company  
**Meta description** (143 characters): The first Polygood® project in Taiwan: furniture and sculpture by ONE DUST Studio, Taipei, in five patterns including Translucent Glitter Gold.

### Text block 1, above the first image gallery

The first Polygood® project in Taiwan is by ONE DUST, a Taipei studio that works with decorative finishes, mineral wall coatings and installation art. Its practice asks how materials connect objects, spaces and nature. Here the studio took its knowledge of surfaces into furniture and sculpture, and Polygood gave it another way into that question, through colour, pattern and form.

### Text block 2, after the image gallery (two paragraphs)

ONE DUST designed and made tables, chairs, benches, shelves and sculptural objects in five Polygood patterns: Maldives, Black Lollipop, Emerald Ghost, Sapphire Terrazzo and Translucent Glitter Gold. This is the only project so far to use Translucent Glitter Gold. Each piece is Polygood through and through: the panels provide both the finished surface and the structure, without a separate supporting frame. The collection moves between speckled terrazzo and translucent colour, and the same material reads differently in each.

Made from recycled plastic, Polygood can be cut, sanded and polished like a solid surface, and shaped into furniture and architectural elements. For a studio this close to its materials, that left room to experiment.

### Text block 3, after the single image A_6853073_1-1

Indoors, the pieces sit against ONE DUST's own mineral finishes, their patterned surfaces beside the earthy textures of the walls and floors. Outside, the work meets its surroundings more directly. A translucent blue panel rises among dark rocks in a gravel garden, with dense greenery beyond. The foliage stays visible through its surface, so the garden becomes part of the piece.
