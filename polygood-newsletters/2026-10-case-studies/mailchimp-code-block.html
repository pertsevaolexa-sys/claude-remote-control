Autumn 2026 · New case studies
Seven patterns in four cities
Under each project you'll find its swatches, named as you'd order them: the Amsterdam plinths are Salmon Terra, PS2121.

In the projects below, Polygood was used for display plinths in an Amsterdam department store, washstands in a Vienna hotel, kitchenette worktops in a Paris office and furniture made by a Taipei design studio.

One pattern, Emerald Ghost, appears in three of them: on the hotel's washstands, on the office worktops and among the studio's pieces.

 
De Bijenkorf · Amsterdam
On Dam Square, Salmon Terra plinths lift mannequins

De Bijenkorf, the Netherlands' premium department store, reopened the women's fashion and shoe department of its Dam Square flagship in 2026, with sustainability in the brief from the first meeting. The design is by Random Studio with the store's own design team, and Fiction Factory built the display stands in Salmon Terra: stepped plinths and blocks that lift mannequins and bags above the shop floor. The store first used Polygood in 2023, for retail fixtures, and chose it again.

When the displays change, the stands can come back through our take-back programme and become new panels.

Application: Stepped display plinths and blocks

Henriette Stadthotel · Vienna
Washstands in two terrazzos and Emerald Ghost

Henriette is a family-run four-star hotel with 68 rooms on Praterstraße in Vienna, a short walk from the Prater. In 2025 its owners, Verena Brandtner-Pastuszyn and Georg Pastuszyn, renovated it as what they call Austria's first Circular Living hotel. Their concept draws on Cradle to Cradle.

In the new Circular Living rooms, the hotel used Polygood for the bathroom washstands, in three patterns: Sapphire Terrazzo, Emerald Ghost and White Terrazzo. The one pictured is Sapphire Terrazzo, with a white basin.

Application: Bathroom washstands

Orion · Paris
Sea-green worktops a shade lighter than teal cabinets

Aura designed the teal kitchenette where Orion's Paris team meets through the day. A white stone or wood top would have cut a line through the teal. The worktops are Polygood Emerald Ghost, a flecked sea green a shade lighter than the cabinets, so the island and counters read as one block of colour. The flecks give the monochrome scheme depth and pick up the speckled stools.

Samji Studio made the worktops and cut the island top to follow its rounded end. The Paris studio has also made Polygood pieces for Adidas.

Application: Kitchenette worktops (island and back counter)

ONE DUST Studio · Taipei
Furniture where the surface is also the structure

Our first project in Taiwan was with ONE DUST, a Taipei studio that makes decorative finishes, mineral coatings and installation art. The studio designed and made tables, chairs, benches, shelves and sculptural objects in five Polygood patterns, among them Translucent Glitter Gold, which no other project has used so far. In every piece the panels form both surface and structure, with no separate frame.

Indoors, the pieces sit against the studio's mineral finishes on walls and floors. In the garden, a translucent blue panel stands among dark rocks, and the forest shows through it.

Application: Tables, chairs, benches, shelves and sculptural objects
