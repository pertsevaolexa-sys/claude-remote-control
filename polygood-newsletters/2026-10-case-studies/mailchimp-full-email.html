On Dam Square, Salmon Terra plinths lift mannequins
Retail
De Bijenkorf, Amsterdam
Application: Display stands: stepped plinths and blocks

De Bijenkorf, the Netherlands' premium department store, reopened the women's fashion and shoe department of its Dam Square flagship in 2026, with sustainability in the brief from the first meeting. The design is by Random Studio with the store's own design team, and Fiction Factory built the display stands in Salmon Terra: stepped plinths and blocks that lift mannequins and bags above the shop floor. The store first used Polygood in 2023, for retail fixtures, and chose it again.

Washstands for the first Circular Living rooms in Austria in two terrazzos and Emerald Ghost
Hospitality
Henriette Stadthotel, Vienna
Application: Bathroom washstands for the Circular Living rooms

In 2025 Verena Brandtner-Pastuszyn and Georg Pastuszyn renovated their family-run four-star hotel on Praterstraße, a short walk from the Prater. They call it Austria's first Circular Living hotel, a concept inspired by Cradle to Cradle.

In the new Circular Living rooms, the hotel used Polygood for the bathroom washstands, in three patterns: Sapphire Terrazzo, Emerald Ghost and White Terrazzo. The one pictured is Sapphire Terrazzo, with a white basin.

Sea-green worktops for an office in Paris
Office spaces
Orion, Paris
Application: Kitchenette worktops (island and back counter)

Aura designed the teal kitchenette where Orion's Paris team meets through the day. The worktops on the island and back counter are Emerald Ghost, a flecked sea green a shade lighter than the cabinets, so the island and counters read as one block of colour. The flecks give the monochrome scheme depth and pick up the speckled stools. Samji Studio, a Paris office-furniture workshop, fabricated the worktops and cut the island top to follow its rounded end.

Surface specialists made furniture and sculpture in Polygood®
Installations
ONE DUST Studio, Taipei
Application: Tables, chairs, benches, shelves and decorative objects

For our first project in Taiwan we worked with ONE DUST, a Taipei design studio that makes installation art and decorative finishes, including mineral wall coatings. Polygood can be cut, sanded and polished like a solid surface. The studio designed and made tables, chairs, benches, shelves and sculptural objects in five patterns: Maldives, Black Lollipop, Emerald Ghost, Sapphire Terrazzo and Translucent Glitter Gold, which no other project has used so far.

Indoors, the colours sit against the studio's own mineral finishes on walls and floors. Outdoors, in a gravel garden backed by forest, a translucent blue slab lets the trees show through.
