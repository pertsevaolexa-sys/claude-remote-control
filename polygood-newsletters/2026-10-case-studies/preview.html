Subject: Four jobs for one recycled panel
Preview text: Plinths in Amsterdam, washstands in Vienna, worktops in Paris, furniture in Taipei
Autumn 2026 · Four new case studies
     
One recycled panel, four different jobs

Polygood® is a panel made from recycled plastic. In the four projects below it became display plinths in an Amsterdam department store, washstands in a Vienna hotel, kitchenette worktops in a Paris office and furniture made by a Taipei design studio.

One pattern, Emerald Ghost, appears in three of them: on the hotel's washstands, on the office worktops and among the studio's pieces.

 
Amsterdam · Retail
De Bijenkorf

De Bijenkorf reopened the women's fashion and shoe department of its Dam Square flagship in 2026. Random Studio, working with the store's own design team, ran a lifecycle assessment workshop before choosing what to build with. The store first used Polygood in 2023 and chose it again. We made the display stands in Salmon Terra: stepped plinths and blocks that lift mannequins and bags above the shop floor, built by Fiction Factory.

Each panel is pressed from one type of recycled plastic, so when the displays change, the stands can come back through our take-back programme and become new panels.

Pattern: Salmon Terra
Application: Display stands: stepped plinths and blocks
See the De Bijenkorf project
 
Vienna · Hotel
Henriette Stadthotel

In 2025 Verena Brandtner-Pastuszyn and Georg Pastuszyn renovated their family-run four-star hotel near the Prater as what they call Austria's first Circular Living hotel. The concept, inspired by Cradle to Cradle, weighs every material on its health, recyclability and carbon, and on its social and biodiversity impact.

For the new Circular Living rooms we made the bathroom washstands in Sapphire Terrazzo, Emerald Ghost and White Terrazzo. The White Terrazzo is made from old fridges and single-use plastic cutlery.

Patterns: Sapphire Terrazzo, Emerald Ghost, White Terrazzo
Application: Bathroom washstands for the Circular Living rooms
See the Henriette project
 
Paris · Workplace
Orion

Aura designed the teal kitchenette where Orion's Paris team meets through the day. A white stone or wood top would have cut a line through the teal. We made the worktops on the island and back counter in Emerald Ghost, a flecked sea green a shade lighter than the cabinets, so the whole kitchenette reads as one block of colour. Samji Studio, which builds office furniture from recycled materials, fabricated the worktops and cut the island top to follow its rounded end.

Pattern: Emerald Ghost
Application: Kitchenette worktops (island and back counter)
See the Orion project
 
Taipei · Design studio
ONE DUST Studio

For our first project in Taiwan we worked with ONE DUST, a Taipei design studio that makes installation art and decorative finishes, including mineral wall coatings. Surfaces are its trade, so Polygood was a natural material for it to try. The studio designed and made tables, chairs, benches, shelves and sculptural objects in five patterns: Maldives, Black Lollipop, Emerald Ghost, Sapphire Terrazzo and Translucent Glitter Gold, which no other project has used so far.

Outside, in a gravel garden backed by forest, a translucent blue slab lets the trees show through.

Patterns: Maldives, Black Lollipop, Emerald Ghost, Sapphire Terrazzo, Translucent Glitter Gold
Application: Tables, chairs, benches, shelves and decorative objects
See the ONE DUST project
