Beyond Subsidy

Dear {{ contact.VORNAME }},

How much of your institution's income depends on a decision made by someone else?

For most museums, the honest answer is: most of it. Subsidies, grants, and public funding are not going away — but they're becoming less predictable, more competitive, and increasingly conditional. The institutions adapting fastest are building income that doesn't reset every fiscal year.

That's what we work on together in Vienna.

What the two days cover

1

Sustainable income models

Revenue streams that compound over time — not ones that reset every grant cycle.
2

Partnerships that generate revenue

Corporate, philanthropic, and cross-sector — structured to go past logo placement.
3

Pricing strategy

Admissions, memberships, licensing — what to charge, when to segment, when to bundle.
4

New value from existing assets

Collections, space, expertise, IP, audience data — what your institution already owns and isn't earning from yet.

This is not a seminar. You arrive with a real institutional challenge and spend two days working on it — with facilitation from expert coaches and input from 24 peers facing the same pressures. You leave with a concrete plan, not a workbook.

The group is capped at 25 participants. That's not marketing — it's how the format works.
