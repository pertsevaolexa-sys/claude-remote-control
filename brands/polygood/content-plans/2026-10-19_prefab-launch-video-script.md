# Polygood pre-fabrication launch: 15-second motion script

**Concept:** from your drawing to the part. A line drawing of a reception desk is plotted onto a Polygood finish. It splits into flat parts, the new service's operations tick off one by one, and the parts come together in the finished pattern.

**Message:** Polygood now prepares the parts before delivery, instead of only shipping full sheets.

**Format:** motion graphics built from existing assets (studio scans, a product render, a line drawing, CNC toolpath lines). No shoot needed. The main cut is Instagram Reels 9:16, with a LinkedIn 4:5 cut below. It works muted, and the voice-over is optional.

**How it was made:** three drafts were written from different angles ("arrives ready", "from your drawing to the part", "material first"). Two judges scored them, one as a social video producer and one as a brand and claims reviewer. Both chose "from your drawing to the part". This version adds the material-first opening and the ticked service list from the other two drafts.

**Read with the sound off:** Start with your drawing → New: we prepare the parts → Cut · machined · edged → Parts, not just sheets → 100% recycled polystyrene → Send your drawing.

**Voice-over:** about 32 words, using the example service list. It must stay within 30–38 words, so keep the spoken service list to 7 words or fewer.

**Text rules:**
- **Length:** each text beat holds for at least 2 s and has 5 words or fewer, not counting the finish tag.
- **Style:** sentence case, no exclamation marks, no hashtags.
- **Placement:** keep text inside the Reels safe area, clear of the bottom caption zone and the buttons on the right.

**Finish:** one finish runs from 0 to 15 s, so the tag is always right. The finish code is on screen whenever the finish is (IG 01 rule on the strategy board).

## Confirm before production

**Service facts.** The boards don't cover the new service yet, so each of these is a placeholder:

| Placeholder | What to confirm |
|---|---|
| [SERVICE LIST] | The operations actually offered. On screen, 5 words or fewer. The toolpaths in beats 2–3 show only confirmed operations: no cut-outs or radiused corners without CNC machining, no finished edge without edging, no curves without V-grooving or thermoforming. |
| [LEAD TIME] · [REGIONS] | Only in the LinkedIn cut and post. Leave them out if they aren't confirmed in writing. |
| [ORDER ROUTE] · [FILE FORMATS] | Where drawings go (form, email or local representative) and which file formats count as a drawing. If a sketch isn't enough, change the CTA to "Send a brief" throughout. |
| [URL] | The landing page or form for drawings, with its own UTM. |
| [PATTERN RANGE] | Whether all 50+ patterns can be ordered as prepared parts. If not, rewrite the beat 4 voice-over. |
| [PS code + pattern name] | The finish in the scan and the render. It must be offered for prepared parts. |
| Thickness | Whether both 12 and 19 mm are offered for prepared parts, and that the edge callout matches the asset. |
| [SERVICE NAME] | Only if the service gets a name. "New: we prepare the parts" works without one. |
| [LOGO] | Which lockup to use: Polygood® or The Good Plastic Company. |

**Claims kept out until operations confirms them:**
- **"Arrives ready to install" and "less to do on site"** were in one of the drafts, but they promise a delivered state nobody has confirmed. Use them only once operations signs off; until then the safe line is "Arrives as parts".
- **Assembly:** beat 4 shows the parts assembled into a desk. If that suggests Polygood assembles furniture, lay the parts flat instead.
- **Fabricators:** confirm whether fabricators can order the service too. The copy should read as less sheet handling, never as replacing the fabricator.

**Assets:**
- **Desk render:** check that a reception desk render and a matching line drawing exist. Otherwise switch to a counter or retail fixture render that does.
- **Scan:** a studio scan of the same finish, at macro quality for the opening.
- **Generic renders:** never present them as client projects.
- **Nesting:** don't show a whole desk nesting into one 2800 × 1400 mm sheet unless it really fits. Show as many sheets as the job needs.

**Exclusions** (checked against the Company Knowledge Board and the content board):
- No take-back (the terms aren't in writing yet).
- No competitors, certifications, carbon numbers or fire ratings.
- No pattern linked to a specific waste stream.

**Licences:** music and sound effects must be cleared for use on Instagram and LinkedIn.
