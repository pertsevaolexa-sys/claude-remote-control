# Polygood prefabrication: 15-second motion script

**Concept:** from your drawing to the part. A line drawing of a tabletop and a bench is plotted onto a Polygood finish. It is cut, edged, machined and curved in-house, and arrives as parts.

**Message:** Polygood prefabricates in-house. Your fabricator can still work the panels like wood, MDF or a solid surface, but when the design calls for it, Polygood can cut, edge, machine and shape the parts before they ship.

**This is not a new service.** [polygood.com/prefabrication](https://polygood.com/prefabrication/) has been online since about mid-2024, and no launch was found. The script presents prefabrication as an existing service and doesn't say "new". Add "New:" to beat 2 only if the service has been relaunched or extended.

**Format:** motion graphics built from existing assets: studio scans, product renders, a line drawing and CNC toolpath lines. No shoot needed. The main cut is Instagram Reels 9:16, with a LinkedIn 4:5 cut below. It works muted, and the voice-over is optional.

**Facts:** every service fact is taken from polygood.com/prefabrication, checked word for word by two independent searches (the page itself can't be opened from this environment). The panel facts come from the Company Knowledge Board and polygood.com/product.

**Read with the sound off:** Start with your drawing → Prefabricated in-house → Your edge, your profile → Curved without thermoforming → Lighter to ship, less waste → Send your drawing.

**Voice-over:** 35 words. Keep it within 30–38.

**Text rules:**
- **Length:** each headline has 5 words or fewer and holds for at least 2 s. The checklist, profile labels and finish tag are small labels, not headlines.
- **Style:** sentence case, no exclamation marks, no hashtags.
- **Placement:** keep text inside the Reels safe area, clear of the bottom caption zone and the buttons on the right.

**Finish:** one finish from 0 to 15 s, so the tag is always right. The cut-out partition in beat 4 uses the same finish, or the tag changes with it.

## Still to confirm before production

**Facts not on the page:**
- **Lead time, regions and minimum order:** none of these is stated. Leave them out unless sales confirms them.
- **File formats:** the downloads page has a "Polygood® Technical Drawing Requirements" PDF. Check which formats it accepts before saying "send your drawing" means a DWG, DXF or sketch.
- **Still current?** The page is about two years old. Check with operations that all four services are still offered as described, including offcut recycling.

**Placeholders:**
- [PS code + pattern name]: the finish in the scans and renders.
- [LOGO]: Polygood® or The Good Plastic Company lockup.

**Wording to get right:**
- **Cut-outs aren't kerfs:** don't call the cut-out designs "kerf cuts". Kerf bending (grooves on the back) is described on the product page, not the prefabrication page.
- **Edge profiles:** the product page has a shorter list (bullnose, bevel, ogee, angled), so don't mix the two lists on screen.
- **Offcuts:** "Offcuts become new panels" refers to prefabrication offcuts recycled in-house. It is not the take-back programme, which is still kept out of social posts until its terms are in writing.
- **Fabricators:** the copy presents prefabrication as an option alongside fabricators, never as a replacement for them.

**Assets:**
- **Line drawing and renders:** a tabletop-and-bench line drawing with matching part renders, and a cut-out partition render.
- **Generic renders:** never present them as client projects.
- **Nesting:** show the parts nested only in a way that fits a 2800 × 1400 mm sheet.

**Licences:** music and sound effects must be cleared for Instagram and LinkedIn.

## Notes for the web and content teams

- **Outdated contact details:** the prefabrication page still lists hello@thegoodplasticcompany.com and old phone numbers. The current contact page uses hello@polygood.com.
- **Thermoforming temperature conflict:** the Company Knowledge Board gives 230–240 °F, while the 2025 patterns guide gives 100–140 °C (212–284 °F). Fix it before anything quotes a temperature, including card TR-LI-04 in the trend-posts file.
