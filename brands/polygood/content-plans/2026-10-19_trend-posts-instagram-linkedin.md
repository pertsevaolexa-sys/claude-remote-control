This week's niche trends (11–18 Oct), turned into post cards for Polygood's own strategy. The cards follow these boards:

- [Polygood Social Media Persona & Platform Strategy 2026](https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVHxmXqzU=/): personas, franchises, operating principles, 90-day plan

Every card was drafted against the boards, then checked against them by a second reviewer. Codes are new (`TR-` for trend), so they don't collide with PL, MS, SS or AO cards.

## How to read a card

The first five lines follow the post-bank layout (frames 03–06): **code · format — personas**, hook, Visual, Ask, Needs. Under them are the fields the Content Library says a card needs before it can be scheduled: franchise, SCAMPER letter, job to be done. After those come the trend it uses, the slot, the board cards it builds on, a caption draft and what to check before posting.

As on the board, a filled Needs line means the post waits on that item. Where something on the board is BLOCKED, the card says so.

## Rules that apply to every card

These come from the boards and the checks:

- **No hashtags in captions yet.** Frame 6.1 (keywords and hashtags) is still TO FILL. Write finish codes without `#` in captions (PS1801, not #PS1801), so they don't turn into hashtags. Keep the `#PS…` lockup on screen.
- **Name the finish code on screen** (IG 01 shoot rule).
- **Polygood facts only from the Company Knowledge Board.**
  - Never link a pattern to a specific waste stream unless production confirms it.
  - State no Cradle to Cradle level.
  - Never say "scratch-proof".
  - Give lifespan only as "over five years in real-world settings".
- **Events:** don't claim Polygood is at Dutch Design Week, ARCHITECT@WORK, Orgatec or Dubai Design Week. Don't spell out what R.A.W. stands for (sources disagree).
- **Competitors:** don't name or discuss Smile Materials, Le Pavé or Plasticiet, in posts or in replies.
- **Content Library's three rules:**
  1. One source asset, many platform-native outputs.
  2. If it only sells, it does not go out.
  3. Anything checkable beats any adjective.
- **Tone of voice is not defined yet.** Frame 1.2 is TO FILL. The drafts use polygood.com's style: sentence case, short titles, no exclamation marks.
