## Caption

Grout is usually the first thing to go.

Not the tile — the line between the tiles. It holds moisture, it discolours, it
collects whatever the room throws at it, and in a busy washroom it gets re-done
long before anything else does.

Polygood® Wall Tiles take the joint out of the equation. The tile pattern is
CNC-machined straight into a single 2800 × 1400 mm panel, so the field you
specified arrives as one continuous surface. Nothing to seal, nothing to stain,
nothing to replace.

The panel is 100% recycled polystyrene — fridge insulation, electronics
housings, CD cases, food containers — and 100% recyclable again at the end of
its life. Cradle to Cradle Certified® Bronze, with a verified EPD behind it.

12 or 19 mm. 50+ colourways. Built for bathrooms, kitchens, hospitality and
anywhere that takes a beating.

Swipe for the spec. Samples at polygood.com
