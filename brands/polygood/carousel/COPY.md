## Instagram caption

Polygood® Wall Tiles are large-format panels of 100% recycled polystyrene with a
tile pattern cut into the surface. This is the first time we've added real,
physical texture to a Polygood surface. Until now the visual pattern mostly came
from the material's own speckle; engraving opens up a whole new design language
for us.

The grooves are precision-machined into a full slab until the field reads as
tile. The panel underneath stays continuous, so a wall that appears to have a
hundred joints has none, and there is no grout anywhere in it. It installs like
a panel, performs like a solid surface, and reads like tile.

The material is solid and through-colour with no surface coatings on it, so a
trolley knock shows the same colour underneath instead of exposing a substrate.
Available in 6, 8 and 12 mm, with 12 mm made for high-impact environments.

Four engravings are available from launch: [1], [2], [3], [4]. Beyond those, the
pattern can be yours. Custom engraving works the way custom patterns have always
worked at Polygood. Send us your brief and we'll create a bespoke engraving
specifically for your project.

## LinkedIn post

Polygood® Wall Tiles are here.

Large-format panels of 100% recycled polystyrene, with the tile pattern engraved
into the surface by CNC. The relief is machined into solid material, so a wall
that reads as a hundred joints is one continuous sheet. Because it's a single
continuous sheet with no grout lines, there's nowhere for moisture to collect,
and nothing to change in a few years.

What that changes for a project:

· Installs as slabs, not as tiles. A 4 to 5 m² run takes one to two hours
  instead of twelve to eighteen.
· Installed labour lands at roughly a quarter of ceramic's. Material cost is
  comparable, so the saving sits in the installation and in the refurbishment
  you don't end up scheduling.
· Around half the weight of ceramic per square metre, which changes what a
  partition can carry and what a shipment costs.
· Solid and through-colour with no surface coatings, so impact damage doesn't
  expose a different colour underneath.
· About 3.7 kg CO₂e per m², which is 14 to 21 kg per m² below ceramic tile.

The colour comes from the plastic itself, so every finish still carries the
waste stream it was made from: refrigerator insulation, CD cases, single-use
cutlery, household electronics.

Four engravings are available from launch: [1], [2], [3], [4]. Beyond those, the
pattern can be yours. Custom engraving works the way custom patterns have always
worked at Polygood. Send us the drawing, we'll tell you what the tooling allows,
and we'll cut it. If you're working on a hotel group or a restaurant brand and
the wall should carry something specific, write to [contact] and we'll come back
with what's possible.
