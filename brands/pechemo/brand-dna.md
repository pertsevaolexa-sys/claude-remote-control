BRAND DNA DOCUMENT
==================

BRAND OVERVIEW
--------------
**Name:** Pechemo – Ukrainian Baking ("Pechemo" translates to "We Bake" in Ukrainian)
**Tagline:** "We Bake Ukraine"
**Sub-tagline:** "Step-by-step recipes, video tutorials, and ingredient calculators for traditional Ukrainian bakes"
**Design Agency:** Unknown / In-house (no external agency credits found)
**Voice Adjectives:** Warm, Accessible, Traditional, Instructional, Heritage-Focused
**Positioning:** A Ukrainian baking platform dedicated to traditional recipes for modern beginner bakers — making centuries-old family traditions accessible to a new generation, wherever they are in the world.
**Competitive Differentiation:**
- vs. **Olia Hercules** (@oliahercules, 150K+ Instagram): Hercules is an established cookbook author and chef (Mamushka, 100K+ copies sold) with a broader Ukrainian cuisine focus. Pechemo differentiates by being baking-only, beginner-first, and digitally native with interactive tools (ingredient calculator, difficulty filters, video tutorials).
- vs. **Tatyana's Everyday Food** (216K Instagram, 627K Facebook): Covers Russian & Ukrainian cuisine broadly with a polished, American-kitchen aesthetic. Pechemo differentiates with a heritage-authentic Ukrainian visual identity, cultural storytelling, and a tighter focus on baking exclusively.
- vs. **Momsdish**: A broad Russian & Ukrainian cooking resource. Pechemo is narrower in scope (baking only) with stronger cultural branding and a more modern, mobile-first design approach targeting younger audiences (18-24 primary demographic).

VISUAL SYSTEM
-------------
**Primary Font:** Custom serif (used for headlines and emphasis, italic styling for key words like "Ukraine" and "Ukrainian tradition")
**Secondary Font:** System sans-serif stack (ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial, Noto Sans)
**Font Weights:** 500 (medium) for titles, 400 (regular) for body, 700 (bold) for emphasis
**Line Heights:** 1.5 for titles, 1.4 for descriptions

**Primary Color:** #3F3F3F (warm dark charcoal — used for body text and primary UI)
**Secondary Color:** #1A1A1A (near-black — used for headlines and strong text)
**Accent Color – Warm Gold/Amber:** hsl(31, 92%, 45%) ≈ #D98A14 (warning/highlight system, echoes honey-gold brand tone)
**Accent Color – Heritage Green:** hsl(140, 100%, 27%) ≈ #008A2E (success states, echoes Ukrainian cultural palette)
**Background Colors:** #FFFFFF (primary), #F9F9F9 (gray1 subtle sections), hsl(0, 0%, 97.3%) (gray2 cards)
**CTA Color and Style:** Dark text (#1A1A1A) on light background buttons; clean, minimal button styling with subtle hover transitions. Newsletter "Subscribe Free" CTA follows same pattern. No bright-colored CTAs — brand relies on warm photography to draw the eye.

PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION
---------------------
**Lighting:** Warm natural daylight, golden-hour preferred (3200K warm temperature). Side-lit or window-lit. Soft, diffused — never harsh or clinical. Honey-gold cast throughout.
**Color Grading:** Warm amber/golden tones. Slightly desaturated greens and blues. High warmth in midtones. Cream-to-golden shadow tones. High contrast between golden baked goods and neutral backgrounds.
**Composition:** Overhead flat-lay dominant. 45° elevated angles for detail shots. Close-up macro for texture (crumb, layers, crust). Hands-in-frame for lifestyle/action shots. Centered or rule-of-thirds product placement.
**Subject Matter:** Finished Ukrainian baked goods (pampushky, medivnyk, syrnyky, varenyky, makivnyk, pliatsok). Dough in progress. Hands kneading, shaping, dipping. Ingredients arranged on surfaces.
**Props and Surfaces:** Wooden cutting boards, wooden cake stands, linen/cloth napkins in cream and beige, ceramic plates (white and cream), vintage forks and spoons, honey jars and spoons, small bowls of sour cream, fresh herbs (mint, dill), tea cups, embroidered Ukrainian cloths, open recipe books.
**Mood:** Cozy, nostalgic, inviting — like stepping into a Ukrainian grandmother's kitchen on a Sunday morning. Aspirational but attainable. Heritage warmth meets modern simplicity.

PRODUCT DETAILS
---------------
**Product:** Recipes — a digital platform of 50+ traditional Ukrainian baking recipes
**Physical Description (of featured baked goods):**
- *Medivnyk (Honey Cake):* Multi-layered golden-brown cake with thin cream layers visible in cross-section. Decorated with piped patterns on top. Honey drizzle on sliced portions. Dense, aromatic texture.
- *Pampushky (Garlic Bread Rolls):* Soft, round, golden-brown yeast rolls dusted with powdered sugar. Pillowy, airy interior with visible crumb texture. Served in heaps on wooden boards.
- *Syrnyky (Cheese Pancakes):* Golden-brown pan-fried cakes with caramelized exterior and creamy white cottage cheese interior. Served stacked or in rows with sour cream and berries.
**Label-Logo Placement:** "ПЕЧЕМО" / "PECHEMO" appears in brand serif font. Logo positioned bottom-right on ad creatives. Small, non-intrusive watermark on UGC-style content.
**Distinctive Features:** All recipes include difficulty rating (Easy/Medium/Advanced), cook time, serving count, and step count. Beginner-friendly focus with foundational 10-recipe guide.
**Packaging System:** Digital-only. Recipe cards with consistent layout: hero image, title, category badge, difficulty, time, servings, description, step count.

AD CREATIVE STYLE
-----------------
**Typical formats:** Instagram feed (1:1), vertical carousel (4:5), Stories/Reels (9:16). Recipe card carousels, step-by-step tutorials, before/after transformations, ingredient spotlights.
**Text overlay style:** Bold serif headlines in warm charcoal or white (depending on background). Italicized emphasis on key Ukrainian cultural words. Clean, minimal — never cluttered. Handwritten-style captions for UGC content.
**Photo vs illustration:** Photography dominant (95%). No illustration. Occasional decorative Ukrainian embroidery patterns (vyshyvanka motifs) as border accents — as seen in product reference moodboards with traditional red-and-brown cross-stitch borders.
**UGC usage:** iPhone-quality hands-in-frame baking shots. Imperfect, authentic, relatable. Friend-sharing-a-recipe energy. Natural daylight, kitchen counter settings.
**Offer presentation:** Soft-sell educational content. "Browse Recipes," "Tap to See Full Recipe," "Subscribe Free." No discount language or price-driven CTAs. Value proposition is knowledge and heritage, not commerce.
