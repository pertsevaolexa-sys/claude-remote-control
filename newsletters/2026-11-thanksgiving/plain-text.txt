POLYGOOD® · THANKSGIVING EDITION, NOVEMBER 2026

FOUR PROJECTS WE'RE THANKFUL FOR

In the US, it is Thanksgiving week. We are borrowing the occasion to thank the hotels, stores, designers, makers and photographers behind four new Polygood® projects.

TODAY'S MENU
To start: Sea Containers London
Main: De Bijenkorf, Amsterdam
On the side: Paagman, The Hague · Solané, Zurich

The five patterns at this year's table: Reverse Timeless Duo #PS1202, Sapphire Terrazzo #PS1801, Salt Dune #PS1701, Translucent Bronze #PS1302 and Espresso Noir #PS2114. Our favourite kind of leftovers: old refrigerators, computer mice, CD cases and spools, pressed into new panels.

------------------------------------------------------------

CHEERS, LORE GROUP
To start · Hotel · London

SEA CONTAINERS LONDON

Lore Group chose Polygood® for the Thames Suites at Sea Containers London, by room count the largest of its seven hotels. For the 2026 refurbishment, creative director Jacu Strauss drew on the colours of the Thames at dusk.

White-flecked black Reverse Timeless Duo runs along the bar counters and onto the round drawer pulls. Deep blue Sapphire Terrazzo, made from recycled spools, forms the side tables and plinths.

Patterns: Reverse Timeless Duo #PS1202 · Sapphire Terrazzo #PS1801
Sea Containers London · Photography: James McDonald

See project details: https://polygood.com/projects/sea-containers-london/

------------------------------------------------------------

DANK JE WEL, DE BIJENKORF
Main · Retail · Amsterdam

DE BIJENKORF, PRIVATE SHOPPING

In De Bijenkorf's Private Shopping suites at Dam 1, the oval coffee table, the side tables and the reception counter are Polygood® Salt Dune, made from recycled white spools.

Random Studio designed the suites, with interiors by Stooff Interior Projects. Burl-wood panelling and cream upholstery frame the Salt Dune pieces.

Pattern: Salt Dune #PS1701
New photography: Milenka Backx · Design: Random Studio

See project details: https://polygood.com/projects/de-bijenkorf/

ON THE SIDE · ALSO NEW THIS AUTUMN

BEDANKT, PAAGMAN
Paagman, Lange Poten · Bookshop · The Hague
In a bookshop of cobalt blue, salmon pink and mint, the cash desk counter is Polygood® Sapphire Terrazzo. Design: ACES of SPACE and +31 ARCHITECTS. Photography: Fleur Huijsdens.
See Sapphire Terrazzo #PS1801: https://polygood.com/product/sapphire-terrazzo/

MERCI VIELMAL, SOLANÉ
Solané, Zurich · Store and café
The new store and café from the makers of Pelikamo has a glossy cash desk in Polygood® Translucent Bronze, made from recycled CD cases, and a cube in Espresso Noir. Photography: Solané.
See Translucent Bronze #PS1302: https://polygood.com/product/translucent-bronze/
