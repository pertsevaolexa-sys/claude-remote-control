Furniture and objects in five Polygood patterns, made in Taipei

The first Polygood® project in Taiwan is by ONE DUST, a Taipei design studio that makes installation art and decorative finishes, including mineral wall coatings. Surfaces are its trade, so Polygood was a natural material to test: a recycled panel that can be cut, sanded and polished like a solid surface, in patterns that run from terrazzo to translucent.

ONE DUST designed and made a body of work in Polygood: tables, chairs, benches, shelves and sculptural objects in five patterns, Maldives, Black Lollipop, Emerald Ghost, Sapphire Terrazzo and Translucent Glitter Gold. Every piece is Polygood through and through, with no frame or other material, so the panels alone keep it stable. Indoors, the colours sit against the studio's own mineral finishes on walls and floors. Outdoors, a translucent blue slab stands among rocks in a gravel garden backed by forest. With the trees showing through, the recycled panel looks at home in the landscape.

Pattern: Maldives, Black Lollipop, Emerald Ghost, Sapphire Terrazzo, Translucent Glitter Gold
Application: Tables, chairs, benches, shelves, decorative objects
Location: Taipei, Taiwan
